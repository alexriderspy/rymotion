One sentence and a few screenshots in, one video out

Each film below came from a single prompt plus the product's own screenshots. Real products, real numbers.

  1. 01Type a sentence and drop in your screenshots or logo.
  2. 02Pick one of six looks, or let the model choose.
  3. 03Preview it in the browser, then export MP4 or WebM with its own music.

Open source, MIT. Ideas borrowed from OpenMotion and @stackedlol's ASCII prompt. Rendered with HyperFrames.
